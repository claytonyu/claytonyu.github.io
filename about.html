About Me

Hello! I'm a sophomore at Carnegie Mellon University majoring in Computational Biology.

I know computation is in the future of biological research, and I'm interested in contributing to biomedical and pharmaceutical breakthroughs. I'm particularly looking towards the field of immunology, and am open to learning about all the exciting work done in other areas of interest! Apart from biology, I enjoy mathematics, chemistry, and computer science.

My life, at its core, is also full of music. I play the piano and french horn (and guitar, somewhat). From playing Scherezade, to Guys and Dolls, to the Overture of Candide, I've realized there is something deeper that comes from the synergy of a musical ensemble. Currently in CMU's Christian a cappella group, Joyful Noise, I'm excited to share a deeper joy through singing.

Thanks if you read all this way! Feel free to contact me at: claytonjyu@gmail.com
