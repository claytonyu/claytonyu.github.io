My Approach

About My Tutoring

I believe that every student has the capacity to understand STEM subjects — the key is finding the right explanation for how they think. My sessions are patient, structured, and adapted to your pace.

My approach starts with understanding where a student currently is — not just in content, but in confidence. We identify gaps, build a foundation, and work systematically toward mastery. I prioritize deep understanding over shortcuts, because a student who understands why something works can solve any problem.

What to expect: active problem-solving in sessions, custom practice materials, and clear communication with parents about progress. I set honest expectations and celebrate real growth.
